Shopkeepers abandon paper pads as NAM bill takes the counter

By Staff Correspondent · Billing Desk

In cafés and retail counters from Bengaluru to Pune, a quiet revolution is underway. NAM bill — a licensed point-of-sale for Android, web, and Windows — lets staff tap a menu, apply GST, and dispatch a WhatsApp PDF invoice before the customer reaches the door.
